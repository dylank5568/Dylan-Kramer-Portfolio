Career objective

I want to build a career in cybersecurity governance, risk, and compliance, and eventually become a Chief Information Security Officer. Working in GRC at Kingland showed me that good security depends on people understanding why a control exists, what risk it addresses, and whether it actually works.

To get there, I plan to keep deepening my technical knowledge and my GRC experience. I also want to get better at explaining technical risk to people without a technical background, because a CISO has to be both a security expert and a clear communicator.
